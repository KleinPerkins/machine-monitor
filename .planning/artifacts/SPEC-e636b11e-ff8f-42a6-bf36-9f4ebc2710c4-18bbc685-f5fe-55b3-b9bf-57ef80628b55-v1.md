# Private delivery CPU reliability acceptance

## Authority and scope

This is a narrowly scoped successor to frozen SPEC-e636b11e-ff8f-42a6-bf36-9f4ebc2710c4-c16a1d3f-c6cd-51cd-acc1-3a8dc92cda7b, version 1, digest sha256:3c424485387e966fa92db9cf397dd8311f5488a3f978eb7e0052edca29bb1762. Incorporate that predecessor's complete requirements by immutable reference. Replace only its production CPU acceptance predicate and the evidence-reassessment rule stated below. This successor applies only to Machine Monitor private delivery; it grants no fleet-wide policy or hosting change. All frozen predecessors remain byte-identical.

Keep the approved PRD, individual authentication and revocation, current-primary D1 authorization on every protected request, descriptor-v2 / 256 KiB transport, full metadata validation, exact EOF/size/cancellation/deadline handling, no public fallback, signatures, private staging, scan-safe admission, stock Sparkle, Recovery, six CI shards, approved source merge process, owner installation, human acceptance and separate stable activation/canary requirements unchanged.

## Platform limits and hard zero billing

Workers Free remains mandatory. Cloudflare's advertised per-request CPU limit remains 10 ms; this specification does not raise the platform limit. Cloudflare documents built-in isolate flexibility for infrequent overruns, without promising a particular burst allowance. A 20 ms acceptance ceiling is our release criterion, not an entitlement to 20 ms from Cloudflare. CPU-limit termination can still produce error 1102 and temporarily prevent an update.

Preserve positive account-bound and timestamped Free verification before provider operations. Unknown or paid state refuses writes. Never activate a paid plan, R2 subscription, payment checkout, metered upgrade or automatic billing. Retain all Free quotas and unconditional Worker-first authorization. Quota/CPU/runtime failures deny delivery without exposing private assets. This decision accepts an availability tradeoff; it introduces no approved cost exposure. An independent future account-owner upgrade or provider pricing change remains outside any zero-billing guarantee.

## Production acceptance predicate

For the same full deployed-version observation window required by the predecessor, require all of:

- Observed CPU P99 is strictly greater than 0 and strictly less than 10,000 microseconds.
- Observed CPU P99.9 is strictly greater than 0 and strictly less than 20,000 microseconds.
- Observed memory P99.9 is strictly greater than 0 and strictly less than 128 MiB.
- The provider reports zero observed Worker execution errors for the window, including resource-limit failures; missing, malformed or unknown observations cannot pass.
- Both complete authenticated archive/package hash rounds, actual private older-to-newer upgrade, dormant relaunch, standalone Recovery, bounded visibility/scan behavior and required resource/credential cleanup pass for the exact candidate.

Require requests_estimate >= 2 * (2 + ceil(archive_size / 262144) + ceil(first_install_size / 262144)), bound to the exact archive/package sizes and both complete hash-verified rounds. Retain exact single-version-at-100% / deployment / source / artifact binding, full-window start at deployment creation, end after actual isolated upgrade/Recovery cleanup, and at least 305 seconds of analytics settling. No intervening deployment, warm-only subset, added dilution traffic, ignored cold requests or exclusion of inconvenient observations is permitted. Keep adaptive sampling/estimation limits explicit. Percentiles and zero observed errors do not guarantee a future maximum or future error-free downloads.

## Immutable evidence reassessment

After this exact successor is approved and delivered through the governed merge process, an existing strict-gate failure may be reassessed once against the new predicate using its original immutable full-window provider response. Preserve the failed receipt, original query/response, observation timestamps and all raw provenance. Do not re-query a better-looking window or call the former 10 ms P99.9 gate passed.

Write a new private reassessment receipt bound to the accepted successor ID/digest, source-delivery evidence, prior failed-receipt and query/response digests, original full-window bounds, exact active deployment/source and archive/package identities, complete functional/cleanup receipts, and explicit new threshold values. State that it is policy reassessment of an existing observation, not a fresh CPU measurement. Refuse missing/changed provenance, deployment/source/artifact mismatches, incomplete functional gates or unknown plan state.

## Existing artifact eligibility

Existing signed candidates may remain eligible only if their actual release code, schema, protocol, packaging inputs and signed archive/package bytes are unchanged, their original source revision is still a verified green merged-main ancestor, all original signing/notarization/stapling/Gatekeeper/signature gates are independently read back, and this governance successor's PR/main checks pass separately. Keep the actual original source revision and signatures; never relabel a binary as built from a later governance commit.

This scoped eligibility allows a policy-only reassessment to retain exact previously tested bytes. Compare the original candidate source revision with the accepted successor merged revision and with live main at reassessment: every changed path across those source deltas must belong only to the reviewed governance transaction and its generated evidence. Independently compare the actual release-code, schema, protocol, build/dependency/configuration and packaging input digests; bind the comparison endpoints and results to the reassessment receipt. Any runtime, build/dependency/configuration, protocol, packaging or binary change requires fresh signed older/newer candidates, exact-source CI, complete readbacks and repeated native/Recovery and production qualification. This exception permits retaining a previously validly prepared artifact after a governance-only source advance; current-main checks remain unchanged for all new builds. Private candidate hashes, recipient details and machine-specific receipts remain outside public Git.

## Remaining delivery gates and operator risk acceptance

The stricter P99.9 threshold's failed evidence remains a failure under its original rule. Adopting this successor explicitly accepts the new observed percentile ceiling, possible higher individual outliers, and the possibility of a future Free-plan resource-limit failure. Native and browser clients retain all integrity checks and owned partial-file cleanup; monitoring remains usable when delivery is unavailable.

Requirements approval alone does not install, publish or activate anything. After source delivery and valid reassessment, continue the already approved reversible owner installation and actual owner upgrade, installed-app scan/menu/lifetime verification, exact human visual/keyboard/VoiceOver/Dock acceptance, finite qualification-access cleanup, unchanged-byte stable promotion, individual owner access activation and separate nonowner download/revocation canary. Missing human acceptance or any other gate leaves that milestone incomplete.
